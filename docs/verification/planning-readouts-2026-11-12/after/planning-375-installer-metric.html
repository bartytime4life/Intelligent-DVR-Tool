Interpretation limits

  • Planning estimate — assumes ideal focus and no compression. Pixel density estimates horizontal detail across the ground, perpendicular to the camera bearing, using ideal pinhole projection; lens distortion, sensor cropping, focus, lighting, motion blur and video encoding affect real detail.
  • DORI bands use the IEC 62676-4 pixel-density figures (25, 62.5, 125 and 250 px/m) as planning thresholds. They are not a compliance, calibration or recognition-quality claim.
  • DORI columns give centreline ground ranges from the mount along the bearing, clipped to the modeled near and far view before wall shadows. Camera-axis depth, not slant distance, sets projected horizontal detail. The ground view uses ideal pinhole image bounds and modeled wall shadows; density contours retain separate components and holes.
  • Coverage shapes are ground-plane estimates clipped to the planned radial range, with bounded curved-edge approximation, modeled doorway gaps and opaque lintels; exterior glazing stays opaque. Floor slabs, furniture, terrain, vegetation and lens effects are not modeled.
  • Positions are meters east and north of the plan origin on a local planning grid, not a survey. Lens presets are typical values; use the lens datasheet.
  • Channel labels are planner notes. This sheet contains no camera credentials, stream addresses or footage.
